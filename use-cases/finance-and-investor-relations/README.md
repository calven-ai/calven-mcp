# Finance and investor relations

Half of what you present isn't financial: why deals are won and lost, whether the pipeline is in profile, what buyers said about price and how the company stacks up against the competitors investors ask about. Calven holds that half, approved by the people who own it and with sample sizes attached, and you ask for it from the AI tool you already use. ARR, cash, margin and CAC stay in your finance stack.
