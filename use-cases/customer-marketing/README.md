# Customer marketing

Your work runs on evidence: who said what, which account proved which outcome, and why a customer bought. Calven holds the verbatim quotes with account, speaker and sentiment, won deals with the reasons buyers gave, the personas each customer maps to, the approved messaging and the product brief with its cross-sell paths. Ask for it from the AI tool you already use. Usage, NPS, support history and contracts live with CS and RevOps.

## Use cases

| Use case | What it gets you |
|---|---|
| [Customer quotes](customer-quotes.md) | Pull verbatim, attributed quotes by theme for pages, decks, ads and reviews |
| [Case studies](../content-marketing/case-studies.md) | Pick the account, build the story from what the buyer said, draft and fact-check (canonical page under content marketing) |
| [Reference program](reference-program.md) | Match a reference customer to a live deal by persona, segment and competitor |
| [Expansion campaigns](expansion-campaigns.md) | Cross-sell and upsell campaigns built on what the account owns and the next product that fits |
| [Advocate identification](advocate-identification.md) | Shortlist customers who talk about outcomes, competitors and switching, in their own words |
| [Release communications](release-communications.md) | Tell customers what changed, in the approved messaging, in the words of the persona who uses it |
| [Customer newsletter](customer-newsletter.md) | A monthly issue built on product changes, customer themes and proof |
| [Review-site program](review-site-program.md) | Who to ask for a review, what to prompt them on, and which quotes already exist |
| [Customer events and webinars](customer-events-and-webinars.md) | Topics, speakers and abstracts built on the pains customers name most |
| [Advisory board prep](../product-management/advisory-board-prep.md) | Agenda, questions and pre-reads from the themes, gaps and competitor moves customers raise (canonical page under product management) |
| [Customer feedback digest](customer-feedback-digest.md) | A monthly read of what customers said, by theme and sentiment, for marketing and CS |
