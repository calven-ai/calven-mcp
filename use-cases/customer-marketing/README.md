# Customer marketing

**Use-case pages:** [`use-cases/customer-marketing/`](../use-cases/customer-marketing/)

## Who they are

One customer marketing manager or a small team inside marketing, reporting to the VP of Marketing or to the CMO, sometimes dotted-line to customer success. They own the relationship between marketing and the installed base: advocacy, references, case studies, reviews, community, customer communications and the campaigns that grow existing accounts. Measured on net revenue retention influenced, reference-able customers, advocates recruited, case studies and reviews published, and engagement in customer programs.

## Responsibilities

**Advocacy and proof**
- Find and recruit advocates: the customers willing to give a quote, a reference call, a review, a talk or a referral.
- Source, interview, draft, approve and publish case studies and testimonials.
- Run the reference program: a pool of reference-able customers matched to live deals, with reference fatigue managed.
- Run review-site programs (G2, Gartner Peer Insights, Capterra, TrustRadius) and the quotes that go in them.
- Recruit customer speakers for webinars, events and press.

**Growth of the installed base**
- Expansion, cross-sell and upsell campaigns, segmented by what the account already owns and the next product that fits.
- Lifecycle and adoption campaigns with customer success: release communications, feature adoption, value-realization materials.
- Renewal support campaigns ahead of contract milestones.

**Engagement and community**
- Customer newsletter and release notes communications.
- Customer community, user groups, customer advisory board.
- Customer events and webinars.
- NPS and survey follow-up: closing the loop with promoters and detractors.

**Measurement**
- Influence on NRR, advocate pool size, reference requests fulfilled, review volume and rating, program engagement.

## Recurring activities

| Cadence | Activity |
|---|---|
| Daily | Reference requests from sales, review responses, advocate conversations |
| Weekly | Case study pipeline review, newsletter and release comms drafting, campaign performance |
| Monthly | Customer newsletter, advocate recruitment pass, review-site push, expansion campaign sends |
| Quarterly | Advisory board or customer event, case study targets, program reporting to marketing and CS leadership, reference pool refresh |
| Per launch | Release communications to customers, adoption campaign, customer quotes for the launch |

## Where Calven fits

Customer marketing lives on evidence: who said what, which account proved which outcome, why a customer bought and what they would say to a peer. Calven holds the verbatim customer quotes with their account, speaker and sentiment, the won deals with the reasons buyers gave, the personas each customer maps to, the approved messaging the stories must support, and the product brief with the cross-sell paths. It does not hold product usage, NPS scores, support history or the customer's contract, so the advocate shortlist and the expansion list start in Calven and get finished with the data CS and RevOps hold.
