# Campaign briefs


A campaign needs the document it runs on: goal, audience, the one idea, messages by persona and stage, proof, channels and assets, and what not to say. You walk away with a brief any writer can produce on-message copy from without a call with the PMM. Calven saves you assembling it from the last deck and a persona slide for every campaign.

## Prompts

### Write the campaign brief

```
Using Calven MCP, write the campaign brief for the campaign below.

FILL IN
- Campaign: [campaign]
- Goal: [pipeline or lead goal]
- Personas: [personas]
- Segment: [segment]
- Dates: [dates]
- Channels: [channels]

CONTEXT
Audience: the personas in the segment. Runs on the dates across the channels. Writers, designers and the agency will work from this without talking to me.

PULL FROM THE UNIVERSE
- The ICP's read on the segment: pains, use cases, buying triggers, disqualifiers.
- The persona canvases for the personas and the messaging matrix for them by stage.
- The top pains, buying triggers and objections customers in this segment raised, with verbatim quotes.
- Proof points and customer quotes that support the campaign's message.
- The product brief entries for anything the campaign mentions.
- Where the personas learn, from the canvases.

BUILD
- The one idea everything ladders up to.
- Audience: who, what they are measured on, the live pain, in their words.
- A persona by stage message table: what we say, the proof, the CTA.
- Objections to pre-empt and the approved response.
- Language guide: ten phrases to use (from quotes), five to avoid.
- Claim boundaries: what the brief allows and what it does not.
- Channels and assets with the message each carries.

OUTPUT
A campaign brief document with sources, plus a one-page summary for the agency.

GROUNDING
Ground every message, quote and claim in the Universe and cite it. Do not invent personas, stages or proof. Flag any gap the team must fill.
```

### Find the strongest campaign angle

```
Using Calven MCP, find the strongest angle for a campaign aimed at the persona in the segment below.

FILL IN
- Persona: [persona]
- Segment: [segment]
- Window: [time window, e.g. last quarter]

PULL FROM THE UNIVERSE
- Themes with the most mentions for this persona in the window, by category (pain, trigger, job).
- Buying triggers and costly objections from the voice-of-customer read.
- Trends relevant to the segment with high severity.

BUILD
Three candidate angles: the pain or trigger, how often it appears, the quote that proves it, the trend that makes it timely, and the pillar it leads to. Recommend one.

OUTPUT
The three angles and the recommendation.

GROUNDING
Counts from the dashboards with n. Quotes verbatim. Do not invent an angle the evidence does not support.
```

### Set the competitive frame

```
Using Calven MCP, set the competitive frame for a campaign aimed at the customers of the competitor below.

FILL IN
- Competitor: [competitor]

PULL FROM THE UNIVERSE
- The competitor's battlecard: where we win, landmines, objection handling.
- Why buyers switched from them, from won-deal drivers and quotes.

BUILD
- The three switching reasons, in the buyer's words.
- The two landmines a campaign can set without naming the rival.
- What not to claim.

OUTPUT
A half-page frame for the brief.

GROUNDING
Only the battlecard and deal evidence, cited.
```

### Check an agency brief against the story

```
Using Calven MCP, review this agency brief against our approved story.

FILL IN
- Brief: [paste the brief]

PULL FROM THE UNIVERSE
- Our ICP, the persona canvases named, the messaging matrix, the product brief.

CHECK
- Audience description vs the ICP and canvases.
- Messages vs the matrix; claims vs the brief.
- Language vs how customers talk.

OUTPUT
The brief annotated, then the corrected version.

GROUNDING
Judge only against the Universe and cite each flag.
```

## Ad hoc questions

- What is the top pain for [persona] in [segment] this quarter, with a quote?
- Which buying triggers appear most in [segment]?
- What is our value proposition for [persona] at the consideration stage?
- Give me three proof points for [pillar].
- Which objections should a [segment] campaign pre-empt?
- What phrases do customers use for [topic]?
- Where does [persona] learn: which events, communities, publications?
- Is [segment] Tier 1 in our ICP?
- What are the disqualifiers for [segment]?
- Which claim can I make about [capability] in an ad?
- What is the one-liner for [product]?
- What trend makes [topic] timely for [segment]?
