# Demand generation

## Who they are

A Director or Head of Demand Generation with campaign, email and paid specialists, often one marketing operations person, plus agencies and freelancers for creative and paid. They report to the CMO or VP Marketing. They are measured on pipeline sourced and influenced, MQLs and SQLs, cost per opportunity, conversion by stage, and the share of target accounts engaged. They write or brief most of the copy the company puts in front of buyers, and they are the heaviest consumer of the PMM's story.

## Responsibilities

**Campaign strategy and planning**
- Set quarterly campaign goals by segment and persona, tied to the pipeline target
- Pick the channel mix: paid search and social, email, webinars, events, content syndication, partners
- Write the campaign brief every writer, designer and agency works from

**Programs**
- Email nurtures and lifecycle programs (lead, trial, onboarding, win-back)
- Paid media: audiences, ad copy, landing pages, budget by segment
- Account-based marketing: target account lists, account plans, personalised plays with sales
- Webinars and events: topics, abstracts, speakers, promotion, follow-up
- Launch campaigns with product marketing
- Social and community posts in the campaign calendar

**Conversion and operations**
- Landing pages, forms, CTAs, A/B tests
- Lead qualification criteria, scoring and routing with sales and RevOps
- Attribution, funnel reporting and campaign retrospectives

**Alignment**
- Weekly reviews with sales on target accounts, MQL quality and follow-up
- Content requests to content marketing and PMM; agency briefs

## Recurring activities

| Cadence | Activity |
|---|---|
| Daily | Campaign performance checks, copy and creative reviews, ad and email edits |
| Weekly | Pipeline and funnel review, sales alignment on accounts and MQLs, content calendar, agency stand-up |
| Monthly | Campaign retrospectives, nurture performance review, budget reallocation, new campaign briefs |
| Quarterly | Campaign plan by segment and persona, ABM target list refresh, lead scoring review, event calendar |
| Per launch | Launch campaign plan, landing page, email sequence, paid and social, follow-up |

## Where Calven fits

Every campaign starts with four questions the Universe answers: who are we targeting, what do they care about at this stage, what do we say and what can we prove. The approved ICP, personas, messaging matrix, proof points and customer language make the brief in minutes and keep every writer on the same story. Persona review tests copy before budget goes behind it. The product brief and claims register keep the ad, the page and the email true. Audience building in the ad platform, the automation tool, design and performance data stay where they are.
