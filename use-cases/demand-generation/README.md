# Demand generation

Every campaign starts with the same four questions: who you're targeting, what they care about at this stage, what you say and what you can prove. Calven holds the approved ICP, personas, messaging matrix, proof points, customer language, product brief and claims register. Ask for them from the AI tool you already use, and test copy against your personas before budget goes behind it. Ad platforms, automation, design and performance data stay put.
