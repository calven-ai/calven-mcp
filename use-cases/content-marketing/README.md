# Content marketing


## Who they are

A content lead or head of content, one to three writers or editors, often a freelancer bench and an agency for SEO. They report to the VP or Director of Marketing. In a small B2B company the PMM or the demand-gen manager carries the role part time. They are measured on organic traffic and rankings, content-sourced pipeline, assets used by sales, and increasingly on how often AI assistants cite the company. Their constant problem is distance from the buyer: the writer rarely sits on a sales call, so pains, objections and proof arrive second hand or not at all.

## Responsibilities

**Strategy and planning**
- Own the content strategy: themes, formats, channels, the quarterly editorial calendar
- Map content to personas and funnel stages, find the gaps, decide what to build next
- Keyword and topic research, search intent, AEO (being the source AI assistants cite)
- Reserve capacity for reactive content: launches, competitor moves, sales requests

**Production**
- Write and edit blog posts, guides, reports, comparison pages, answer pages, newsletters
- Brief and manage writers, freelancers and agencies; keep their output on-message
- Produce case studies with customers: interview, draft, approval, publish
- Webinar programs: topic, abstract, speakers, promotion copy, follow-up assets
- Repurpose long-form into social, email and sales assets

**Quality and governance**
- Fact-check product, pricing and competitor claims before publishing
- Keep the content library current: audits, refreshes, merges, redirects
- Brand voice and messaging consistency across writers and channels
- Legal and claims review for comparison and competitive content

**Measurement**
- Traffic, rankings, AI citations, conversions, content-sourced pipeline
- Which assets sales uses, which ones buyers mention on calls

## Recurring activities

| Cadence | Activity |
|---|---|
| Daily | Writing and editing, reviewing freelancer drafts, answering sales requests for an asset |
| Weekly | Editorial stand-up, publishing one to three pieces, social and newsletter copy, briefing writers |
| Monthly | Performance review, content refresh batch, case-study interviews, webinar prep |
| Quarterly | Editorial plan, content audit and gap analysis, keyword and topic refresh, claim review after releases |
| Per launch | Launch content set: announcement post, feature pages, comparison updates, FAQ |
| Per competitor move | Comparison page update, response post |

## Where Calven fits

Every piece of content needs the same four things the writer does not have on hand: who it is for and what they are measured on, the words buyers use for the problem, the approved message and proof, and what the product actually does. Calven holds all four as approved, cited documents and records, so the writer's AI tool drafts from the company's own evidence instead of generic category copy. Keyword data, rankings, analytics and the content library itself stay in the SEO and CMS tools. Calven does not search the web.
