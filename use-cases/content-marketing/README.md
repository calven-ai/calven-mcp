# Content marketing

Every piece you write needs four things you rarely have on hand: who it's for and what they're measured on, the words buyers use for the problem, the approved message and proof, and what the product actually does. Calven holds all four, approved and cited, so the AI tool you already use drafts from your company's evidence instead of generic category copy. Keywords, rankings and the CMS stay in your SEO and content tools.
