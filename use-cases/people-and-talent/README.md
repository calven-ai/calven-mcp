# People and talent

You screen every GTM hire on whether they get the buyer, the market and the competition, and every new hire needs the company story in week one. Both come from the approved ICP, personas, positioning, messaging, product brief and battlecards, which Calven holds and keeps current. Ask for them from the AI tool you already use to build scorecards, interview briefs, onboarding packs and quizzes. Headcount, comp, HRIS and ATS data stay in your people stack.
