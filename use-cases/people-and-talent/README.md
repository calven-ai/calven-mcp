# People and talent

## Who they are

A recruiter or talent partner, a people operations lead, and the hiring managers they work with. At a small company it is the founder plus an agency. They are measured on time to fill, quality of hire, ramp time to productivity, offer acceptance, early attrition and whether new hires can tell the company story by the end of week one.

## Responsibilities

**Hiring**
- Role intake with the hiring manager: scorecard, must-haves, competencies
- Job descriptions and the careers page; sourcing and outreach
- Structured interviews: screen, competency interview, practical exercise, leadership round, with a shared rubric
- For GTM roles: a mock discovery call or pitch built from a brief shared in advance; a pipeline or territory case
- Offer, close, references

**Onboarding and ramp**
- Week-one orientation: company, mission, market, customers, competitors, how we sell
- 30-60-90 plans per role; sales and CS ramp curricula (product, buyers, process, tools, shadowing, certification)
- Knowledge checks and certification on the company story, personas and battlecards

**Employer brand and internal communication**
- Employer value proposition, careers content, candidate-facing narrative consistent with the company's positioning
- Internal communications on the company narrative: all-hands, company updates, the "what we do and for whom" everyone repeats

**Workforce planning and people operations**
- Headcount plans, compensation, performance cycles, HRIS, compliance

## Recurring activities

| Cadence | Activity |
|---|---|
| Daily | Screens, interview scheduling, candidate outreach |
| Weekly | Hiring pipeline review with managers, interview debriefs |
| Per hire | Intake and scorecard, job description, interview kit, case exercise, references, offer |
| Per start | Week-one pack, 30-60-90 plan, buddy, certification |
| Monthly | New-hire cohort onboarding, employer brand content |
| Quarterly | Ramp review, careers page refresh, all-hands narrative |

## Where Calven fits

Every GTM hire is screened on whether they understand the buyer, the market and the competition, and every new hire in any role needs the company story in week one. Both come from the approved ICP, personas, positioning, messaging, product brief and battlecards, which Calven holds and keeps current. The AI tool turns them into scorecards, interview briefs, case exercises, onboarding packs and certification quizzes. Headcount plans, compensation, HRIS and ATS data stay in the people stack.
