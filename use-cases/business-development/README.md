# Business development (BDRs and SDRs)

Almost every email you write depends on what the company knows and rarely hands you: who the persona is, why similar accounts bought or walked, where the competitor in their inbox is weak. Calven holds the persona canvases, customer quotes, deal drivers, battlecards and the CRM mirror with ICP fit. Ask for it from the AI tool you already use, and test a draft on the buyer before it goes out. Your sequencer and CRM stay put.
