# Revenue operations

**Pages:** [`use-cases/revenue-operations/`](../use-cases/revenue-operations/)

## Who they are

RevOps brings sales ops, marketing ops and customer success ops under one function that owns the revenue process, the data and the systems behind it. In a company under 200 people it is one or two people, often reporting to the CRO or the COO; sometimes it is still a sales ops manager plus a marketing ops manager. Measured on pipeline coverage, win rate, forecast accuracy, sales cycle, lead-to-opportunity conversion, quota attainment and CRM data quality.

## Responsibilities

**Planning**
- Annual and quarterly GTM planning: segmentation, territory design, quota allocation, capacity modelling.
- Keep the ICP, the segment tiers and the account scoring current, and rebuild territories when they drift.

**Pipeline and forecasting**
- Run the operating cadence: pipeline reviews, forecast calls, deal reviews, QBRs.
- A standard forecasting method, pipeline health rules, coverage targets by segment.

**Lead management**
- Lead scoring, routing and assignment; the marketing-to-sales hand-off; SLAs and response time.

**Process**
- Stage definitions, exit criteria, hand-offs between marketing, sales and customer success; exception handling.

**Data and systems**
- CRM administration and hygiene, the revenue tech stack, integrations, field definitions, data quality.

**Analytics and reporting**
- Win rates by segment, competitor and persona; lost deal analysis; pipeline and funnel reporting; board and QBR packs; win/loss program health.

## Recurring activities

| Cadence | Activity |
|---|---|
| Daily | Lead routing exceptions, CRM hygiene, rep requests, deal desk |
| Weekly | Pipeline review, forecast call, funnel report, data quality check |
| Monthly | Win rate and conversion review, lost deal analysis, lead scoring tuning, must-win deal review |
| Quarterly | QBR pack, territory and quota review, ICP and segment refresh, stack audit, win/loss readout |
| Annually | Planning cycle: segmentation, territories, quotas, capacity, comp |

## Where Calven fits

RevOps owns the CRM; Calven mirrors its accounts, contacts and deals and adds what the CRM does not have: ICP fit per account with the reason, the approved ICP and segment tiers, win/loss drivers in the buyer's words, win rates by competitor and persona with the sample behind them, and the persona and competitor context behind every deal. From the AI tool, RevOps ranks accounts, builds territory and routing inputs, cuts win rates by segment, explains losses and writes the QBR pack from one source. Territory assignment, routing rules, forecast submission and any CRM edit stay in the CRM and the tools that drive it. Marketing attribution is not in Calven.
