# Awards submissions

**Team:** Brand and communications · also customer marketing, product marketing
**Impact:** Medium. An award entry is a case study with a scoring rubric. Entries that answer each criterion with a named customer outcome, a verbatim quote and a differentiator on record win; entries written from the website do not. The same evidence serves speaking submissions.
**Prerequisites:** strategy documents approved (positioning, product brief). Better with call transcripts ingested (quotes with Quantified outcome tags), win/loss surveys running (why customers chose us) and CRM connected (the account and the deal).

## What the team is trying to do

Submit to the two or three awards a year that buyers and analysts notice, with entries that hit every criterion and survive a judge who checks. Done means an entry built on customers, numbers and differentiators that exist, approved by the customer, submitted on time. The hard part is assembling proof across calls, deals and documents under a deadline.

## The work, end to end

| # | Step | What the team does | Where Calven helps | Pulls from |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Choose the award and category | Which fits | Our positioning and ICP, to match the category | Positioning, ICP |
| 2 | Map the criteria to evidence | What each criterion needs | For each criterion: the differentiator, the customer outcome, the quote, the number on record | Positioning, quotes, surveyed deals, Insights |
| 3 | Pick the customer story | The account that proves it | Accounts with Quantified outcome quotes in the right segment | Quotes, CRM accounts |
| 4 | Draft the entry | Problem, approach, result, differentiation | Draft against the criteria from steps 2 and 3 | All of the above |
| 5 | Check | Claims and quotes | Claim check against the brief and the record | Product brief, claims, quotes |
| 6 | Customer approval and submission | Sign-off, upload | Calven does not help here | |

## Recommended prompts

### Step 2 and 3: criteria to evidence

```
Using Calven MCP, map the criteria of [award, category] to our evidence.

CONTEXT
Below are the entry criteria and word limits. I need, for each criterion, what we can prove.

PULL FROM THE UNIVERSE
- Our positioning: unique attributes, value themes, proof points.
- Customer quotes tagged Quantified outcome, Time-to-value, Competitive win and Consolidation, with account and approval state.
- Surveyed deals with a positive summary and the win drivers behind them.
- The product brief for any capability claim.

BUILD
- A table: criterion, the differentiator or outcome that answers it, the evidence (quote, deal, number) with source, and the gap if none.
- The two or three accounts whose evidence covers the most criteria.

OUTPUT
The table and the account shortlist, with sources.

GROUNDING
Use only quotes, deals and documents in the Universe and cite them. Do not invent an outcome or a number; write "no evidence on record" where that is true.

[paste the criteria and name the award]
```

### Step 4: draft the entry

```
Using Calven MCP, draft the [award] entry around [account].

CONTEXT
Below are the criteria with word limits and the evidence map. Customer: [account]. Write to the rubric, plain, no superlatives.

PULL FROM THE UNIVERSE
- Every quote on record from [account], verbatim.
- The deal context for [account].
- Our positioning and the product brief for the capability lines.

WRITE
- One section per criterion, within its limit, leading with the outcome and citing the evidence.
- The customer's words verbatim where a quote is allowed.
- A 50-word summary for the entry's opening.

OUTPUT
The entry in sections, with a source list and the lines the customer must approve.

GROUNDING
Use only the evidence map and the Universe and cite. Do not round numbers up or add capabilities the brief does not list.

[paste the criteria and the evidence map; name the account]
```

### Step 5: check the entry

```
Using Calven MCP, check this award entry before the customer sees it.

CONTEXT
Below is the entry.

PULL FROM THE UNIVERSE
- The product brief and claims register.
- The quotes on record for the customer named.

CHECK
- Every capability and number: correct, wrong, stale or unverified.
- Every quote: verbatim or edited, and its approval state.
- Any "first", "only" or "leading" line: supported by a proof point or to cut.

OUTPUT
The entry annotated and the list for the customer.

GROUNDING
Judge only against the Universe and cite.

[paste the entry]
```

## Ad hoc questions

- Which customers have quotes tagged Quantified outcome in [segment]?
- What differentiators does our positioning claim, with proof points?
- Why did [account] choose us, according to their survey?
- Do we have a Competitive win quote involving [competitor]?
- Which of our proof points has a number behind it?
- Is "[claim]" in the product brief?
- Which customer quotes are approved for marketing use this quarter?
- What was the deal size band and segment for [account]?

## Good practice

- Map criteria to evidence before choosing the customer. The account with the most covered criteria wins.
- Keep quotes verbatim and get the customer's approval on the exact lines.
- Cut any superlative the proof points do not back. Judges check.
- Reuse the evidence map for speaking submissions; the criteria overlap.

## Not covered today

- Award selection beyond category fit, deadlines, fees and the submission portal.
- Customer approval.
- Design assets and videos.
