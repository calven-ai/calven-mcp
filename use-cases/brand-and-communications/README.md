# Brand and communications


## Who they are

A head of communications or brand, a PR manager, a social media manager, sometimes an analyst relations lead, often a PR agency. In a company under 200 people it is one person, or the PMM and the CEO between them. They report to the CMO or the CEO. They are measured on coverage in the publications buyers read, analyst standing, executive and company reach on LinkedIn, share of voice against competitors, and whether every external statement tells the same story. Their constant problem is that they speak for the company before the rest of it has agreed what to say.

## Responsibilities

**Narrative and consistency**
- Own the corporate narrative, boilerplate, one-liner and the messaging every channel repeats
- Keep website copy, press materials, social and executive statements on the approved positioning
- Review external statements for claims, tone and competitive risk

**Media and analyst relations**
- Press releases for launches, funding, customers, partnerships, hires
- Media pitches, spokesperson briefings, interview prep, press kit
- Analyst briefings, inquiries, evaluation submissions, capturing analyst feedback for the company

**Executive and social voice**
- Executive thought leadership: LinkedIn posts, bylines, keynote abstracts, podcast prep
- Company social channels: calendar, posts, launch and event coverage, community replies
- Awards and speaking submissions

**Response**
- Competitive response when a rival launches, reprices or attacks
- Issue and crisis statements, customer-facing holding statements, internal talking points

## Recurring activities

| Cadence | Activity |
|---|---|
| Daily | Social posts and replies, media monitoring, executive post drafts |
| Weekly | Editorial review of social calendar, pitch outreach, executive content session, statement reviews |
| Monthly | Analyst briefing or inquiry, awards and speaking deadlines, coverage report |
| Quarterly | Narrative and boilerplate refresh, messaging consistency audit across channels, analyst evaluation cycles |
| Per launch | Press release, pitch list, spokesperson prep, social set, FAQ |
| Per competitor move | Response statement, talking points for sales and support, social stance |

## Where Calven fits

Communications repeats the company's story more often than any other team and gets corrected in public when it drifts. Calven holds the approved positioning, messaging and boilerplate, the product facts, the customer evidence a release or an award entry needs, the battlecards a response statement draws on, and the trends an executive's point of view should anchor on. Media lists, coverage tracking, social scheduling and monitoring stay in the PR and social tools. Calven does not search the web.
