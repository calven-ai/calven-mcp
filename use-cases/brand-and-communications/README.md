# Brand and communications

You tell the company's story more than any other team, and you get corrected in public when it drifts. Calven holds the approved positioning, messaging and boilerplate, the product facts, the customer evidence a release or award entry needs, the battlecards behind a response statement and the trends an exec's point of view anchors on. Ask for it from the AI tool you already use. Media lists, monitoring and social scheduling stay in your PR tools.
