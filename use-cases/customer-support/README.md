# Customer support

You answer from the product and the customer, and a macro written last year doesn't know what changed. Calven holds the approved product brief, pricing and packaging, the competitor battlecards and what other customers say about the same problem, and keeps them current. Ask for it from the AI tool you already use while you write the reply. The ticket system, usage data and help centre stay where they are.

## Use cases

| Use case | What it gets you |
|---|---|
| [Product and capability questions](product-questions.md) | Answer "does it do X" from the approved product brief, including what we do not do |
| [Competitor questions from customers](competitor-questions.md) | "How do we compare to X" answered from the battlecard, honest about where we lose |
| [Feedback routing to product](feedback-routing.md) | Recognise which theme a ticket belongs to, how often it comes up, and what it costs in deals |
| [Escalation summaries](escalation-summaries.md) | Who the account is, the deal and buying group behind it, the competitor in play, before the escalation goes out |
| [Pricing and packaging questions](pricing-questions.md) | What each plan includes and what the approved answer to a pricing objection is |
| [Help-centre articles and macros](help-centre-and-macros.md) | Drafts grounded in the product brief, checked against the claims register |
| [Change and deprecation communication](change-communication.md) | What changed in the product, which articles and macros it left stale, the customer-facing note |
| [New agent training](new-agent-training.md) | A product, ICP, persona and competitor curriculum built from the approved documents |
