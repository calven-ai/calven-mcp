# Solutions engineering

**Use-case pages:** `use-cases/solutions-engineering/`

## Who they are

Solutions engineers (sales engineers, presales) carry the technical half of the deal: they run technical discovery, design and deliver the demo, scope and run the proof of concept, answer the technical questions and the RFP, and secure the technical win so the AE can close the commercial one. They report to a presales leader or the VP Sales and are measured on technical win rate, POC conversion, demo-to-advance rate and deals supported. One SE usually covers several AEs, so the same question arrives from four deals a week and the answer has to be the approved one every time.

## Responsibilities

**Discovery and qualification**
- Run technical discovery: current architecture, integrations, data, security requirements, success criteria
- Map requirements to capabilities and say early what will not fit
- Qualify the technical buyer and the evaluation process

**Demonstration**
- Design the demo storyline per buying group with the AE
- Build and maintain demo environments and data
- Deliver the demo and handle the technical questions live

**Evaluation**
- Scope the proof of concept or pilot: criteria, timeline, named stakeholders on both sides
- Run it, track criteria, close it with a technical win summary
- Handle technical objections and competitive comparisons during evaluation

**Documents**
- Respond to RFPs and RFIs; keep an answer library current
- Answer security questionnaires with the security and legal teams
- Write the technical sections of proposals and SOWs

**Internal**
- Feed what prospects asked for and where the product fell short back to product management
- Train AEs and customer success on the product
- Hand the technical context to implementation and customer success at close

## Recurring activities

| Cadence | Activity |
|---|---|
| Daily | Discovery and demo calls, technical answers by email and in deal channels, demo environment upkeep |
| Weekly | Deal syncs with AEs, POC check-ins, RFP and questionnaire work, pipeline review on SE-supported deals |
| Per deal | Technical discovery, demo design, POC scoping and closeout, technical win summary, handoff |
| Monthly | Product feedback roundup to product management, answer-library updates after releases |
| Quarterly | Demo refresh after releases, enablement sessions for AEs, win/loss review on technical losses |

## Where Calven fits

The SE's raw material is the approved product truth (what the product does, integrates with and does not do), the competitor's product as recorded in the dossier, what technical buyers asked and objected to on calls, and which technical gaps lost deals. Calven holds the product brief, the claims register, product changes, battlecards and feature comparisons, technical-buyer personas, quotes and win/loss drivers, and the SE reads them from the AI tool while writing the answer. Demo environments, configuration depth, the live POC and the security program's own evidence stay with the SE and the engineering and security teams.
