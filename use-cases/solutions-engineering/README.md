# Solutions engineering

Your raw material is the approved product truth, the competitor's product as the dossier records it, what technical buyers asked and objected to, and which technical gaps lost deals. Calven holds the product brief, claims register, product changes, battlecards and feature comparisons, technical-buyer personas, quotes and win/loss drivers. Ask for it from the AI tool you already use while you write the answer. Demos, the POC and security evidence stay with you.

## Use cases

| Use case | What it gets you |
|---|---|
| [Technical discovery](technical-discovery.md) | The questions for the technical buyer, the fit check against the brief, what to rule out early |
| [Demo design](demo-design.md) | The storyline and the capability map for a buying group, built with the AE |
| [POC scoping](poc-scoping.md) | Success criteria tied to the buyer's pains, the trap for the competitor, the closeout summary |
| [Technical objections](technical-objections.md) | "Does it do X", "it does not scale", "it will not integrate", answered from the brief and from deals won |
| [Competitive technical comparisons](competitive-technical-comparisons.md) | Feature-by-feature against a rival, honest about where they are ahead |
| [RFP and RFI responses](rfp-responses.md) | The product, integration and differentiation answers, with the approved wording and the gaps marked |
| [Security questionnaires](security-questionnaires.md) | The product-level answers the brief supports; everything else routed to security |
| [Technical win reports and product feedback](technical-win-reports.md) | The closeout per deal, and the ranked list of gaps that cost deals for product |
