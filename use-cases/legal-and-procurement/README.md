# Legal and procurement

Your recurring question is whether the company can prove a claim and whether it's still true. Calven holds the approved product brief, every claim your documents and site make with its proof status, product changes and the documents they made stale, and the battlecards, dossiers, signals and win/loss behind competitor statements. Check a claim against the source from the AI tool you already use. Contracts, policies and legal judgment stay with you, and none of this is legal advice.

## Use cases

| Use case | What it gets you |
|---|---|
| [Claim substantiation](claim-substantiation.md) | Every claim on the site and in documents with its proof status, and the ones to pull or prove |
| [Comparative content review](comparative-content-review.md) | Check a "vs" page, teardown or competitive ad against what the Universe evidences about the rival |
| [Launch copy review](launch-copy-review.md) | Release notes, press copy and launch pages checked against the product brief and drift findings |
| [Due-diligence answers](due-diligence-answers.md) | Answer a buyer questionnaire only from what the product brief and claims hold, and list what is not there |
| [Order form and proposal fact check](order-form-fact-check.md) | Confirm every inclusion, limit, price and integration a proposal promises |
| [Naming conflict scan](naming-conflict-scan.md) | Check candidate names against tracked competitor names, products and categories before the trademark search |
| [GTM tool procurement](gtm-tool-procurement.md) | What the company already knows about a vendor it is evaluating, when that vendor is tracked or appears in signals |
