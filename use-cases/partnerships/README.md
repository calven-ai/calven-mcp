# Partnerships

Everything a partner says about the company comes from positioning, messaging, the product brief, the ICP and the battlecards, and you're the one handing it over in a form they can use. Calven holds the approved version of each, the win/loss evidence that makes a partner talk track credible, and the trends and opportunities behind a joint value proposition. Ask for it from the AI tool you already use. PRM, portals, contracts and deal registration stay put.

Want partner reps asking these questions themselves? Invite them into the workspace as members. The two Security-page toggles (transcripts, pipeline) apply to every member, so inviting partners also decides whether they see customer calls and CRM data.
