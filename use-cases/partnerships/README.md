# Partnerships

**Use-case pages:** `use-cases/partnerships/`

## Who they are

A head of partnerships or channel, partner managers (resellers, referral partners, implementation partners), alliance managers (technology and integration partners) and, where the program is large enough, a partner marketing manager. They report to the CRO, CMO or CEO. Measured on partner-sourced and partner-influenced pipeline and revenue, number of active partners, time to a partner's first registered deal, partner certification rates and joint-campaign results. They spend their time outside the company: a partner's reps have to pitch, position and handle objections without anyone from the vendor in the room.

## Responsibilities

**Recruiting and qualifying partners**
- Define the ideal partner profile: the segments, verticals, regions and complementary offerings that fit.
- Evaluate candidates on alignment with the ICP, commitment, stability and ability to sell.

**Onboarding and enablement**
- Run the first 30 to 90 days: program orientation, product training, sales enablement, tool access, first-deal support.
- Build the enablement kit: pitch deck, one-pagers, talk tracks, demo scripts, battlecards, pricing guardrails, email templates.
- Certify partner reps and keep the kit current as the product and the market move.

**Co-selling**
- Run deal registration: validate the account, check for conflict, protect the deal, plan the joint approach.
- Map target accounts with the partner, run joint pipeline reviews.

**Co-marketing**
- Agree the joint value proposition and messaging with each partner.
- Plan and run co-branded campaigns, webinars, case studies, marketplace listings against a quarterly calendar.

**Governance**
- Hold quarterly business reviews: pipeline, wins and losses, enablement gaps, what changed in the market, the next quarter's plan.
- Report partner performance to leadership.

## Recurring activities

| Cadence | Activity |
|---|---|
| Daily | Partner questions ("what do we say when the customer asks X"), deal registration reviews |
| Weekly | Joint pipeline reviews, enablement content updates, partner marketing check-ins |
| Monthly | Partner newsletter or update on what changed in product and competition, performance reporting |
| Quarterly | QBRs with every managed partner, joint marketing calendar, partner recruitment review |
| Per new partner | Kickoff briefing, enablement kit, certification, first-deal support |
| Per launch | Partner-facing version of the launch messaging and talk track |

## Where Calven fits

Everything a partner has to say about the company comes from positioning, messaging, the product brief, the ICP and the battlecards. Partnerships is the team that has to hand those over in a form an outsider can use, and keep them current across many partners at once. Calven holds the approved version of each, the win/loss evidence that makes a partner talk track credible, and the market trends and opportunities behind a joint value proposition. Partner portals, PRM systems, contracts and deal-registration tooling are not in Calven.

A partner's own reps can ask these questions themselves once they are invited into the workspace as members. The two Security-page toggles (transcripts, pipeline) apply to every member alike, so a workspace that invites partners decides at the same time whether partners see customer calls and CRM data.
