# Co-marketing briefs

**Team:** Partnerships (partner marketing) · also demand generation, content marketing, product marketing
**Impact:** Medium. Joint webinars, guides and case studies need a brief both companies work from. Written from the company's messaging and the shared persona, the company's half is on-message from the first draft and the partner's team gets what they need in one document.
**Prerequisites:** strategy documents approved (messaging, positioning, product brief), personas approved. Better with call transcripts ingested (customer language and quotes) and market research run (the theme behind the campaign).

## What the team is trying to do

Write the brief for a joint marketing piece: the audience, the joint message, each company's messages, the proof, the asset and the calendar. Done means the partner's marketer can write their half without a call. Partner marketing briefs usually get written in the partner's words because the partner moved first; this keeps the company's half on its own story.

## The work, end to end

| # | Step | What the team does | Where Calven helps | Pulls from |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Agree the asset and goal | Webinar, guide, case study, listing; leads, awareness | Calven does not help here | |
| 2 | Define the audience | The shared persona and segment | The persona canvas and the ICP segment | Personas, ICP |
| 3 | Set the joint message | From the joint value proposition | The joint value proposition if recorded as a document, otherwise positioning and messaging | Positioning, messaging |
| 4 | Pick the theme | What the piece is about | Trends and opportunities the persona cares about; customer pains and jobs in their words | Trends, market opportunities, quotes, themes |
| 5 | Write our messages and proof | The company's half of the brief | Messaging pillars for the persona, proof points, approved quotes | Messaging, positioning, quotes |
| 6 | Draft the abstract and CTA | Webinar abstract, guide outline, case study angle | A draft in the persona's words | Personas, messaging |
| 7 | Review the partner's half | Check the joint draft against our story and facts | Messaging alignment and fact check | Messaging, product brief, claims |
| 8 | Run and measure | Calendar, promotion, results | Calven does not help here | |

## Recommended prompts

### Step 2 to 6: the co-marketing brief

```
Using Calven MCP, write our half of a co-marketing brief with [partner] for a [webinar / guide / case study] aimed at [persona] in [segment].

CONTEXT
The goal is [leads / awareness / pipeline in a segment]. The partner's marketer will add their half. I want ours on-message and in the buyer's words.

PULL FROM THE UNIVERSE
- The [persona] canvas: pains, jobs, watering holes.
- The ICP read on [segment].
- Our messaging: the pillars and value proposition for [persona]; our positioning value themes.
- Trends or opportunities relevant to the theme.
- Customer quotes on the theme, approved for marketing.

BUILD
- Audience: who, what they are trying to do, where they learn.
- The theme and why now, grounded in a trend.
- Our three messages and the proof behind each.
- A draft abstract or outline in the persona's words.
- The CTA.
- What we need from the partner.

OUTPUT
The brief in that order with sources.

GROUNDING
Use only the Universe for our side. Do not write the partner's messages. Keep quotes verbatim and attributed as approved.

[name the partner, asset, persona and segment]
```

### Step 6: a webinar abstract

```
Using Calven MCP, write a webinar abstract for a joint session with [partner] on [theme] for [persona].

CONTEXT
120 words, a title, three takeaways. Must read as a problem the persona has, not two vendors talking.

PULL FROM THE UNIVERSE
- The [persona] canvas pains and jobs on this theme.
- The words customers use for it, from calls.
- The trend behind it, if tracked.

WRITE
- Title, abstract, three takeaways.

OUTPUT
The abstract with sources.

GROUNDING
Use only canvas, quotes and trends in the Universe. Do not promise outcomes the product brief does not support.

[name the partner, theme and persona]
```

### Step 7: review the joint draft

```
Using Calven MCP, check this joint piece against our messaging and product facts.

CONTEXT
Below is the draft after the partner's edits.

PULL FROM THE UNIVERSE
- Our messaging and positioning.
- The product brief and the claims register.

CHECK
- Lines about us that are off-message or overstated.
- Product claims not in the brief.
- A category frame we do not use.

OUTPUT
The draft annotated, then the clean version of our lines only.

GROUNDING
Judge only our lines against the Universe. Leave the partner's lines alone.

[paste the draft]
```

## Ad hoc questions

- What does [persona] care about that both we and [partner] solve?
- Where does [persona] learn, according to the canvas?
- Which trend should a joint webinar with [partner] be built on?
- Give me three customer quotes on [theme] approved for marketing.
- What is our value proposition for [persona] at the awareness stage?
- Which pillar fits a joint case study with [partner]?
- Is "[claim in the partner's draft]" something our brief supports?
- What is our approved boilerplate for a co-branded piece?
- Which segment should a joint campaign target first, from the ICP tiers?
- How do customers describe [theme] in their own words?

## Good practice

- Write your half before you read the partner's. It stays on your story.
- Lead with the persona's problem and the trend, not the integration.
- Give the partner the watering holes from the canvas. It settles the promotion plan.
- Review after every partner edit. Joint drafts drift one line at a time.

## Not covered today

- The partner's messages and proof.
- Promotion, registration, lead routing, results.
- Design and production.
- Co-marketing budget and MDF.
