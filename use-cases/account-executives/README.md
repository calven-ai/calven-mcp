# Account executives

Most of what you prep before a call is stuff the company already knows and nobody has time to dig up: how similar deals were won, what sank the last few against this competitor, what this buyer's persona cares about. Calven holds the approved positioning, battlecards, persona canvases, win/loss evidence, customer quotes and the CRM mirror, and you ask for it from the AI tool you already use. The call, the negotiation and the CRM update are still yours.
