# Calven MCP use cases

Connecting Calven to your AI tool is the quick part. Knowing what to ask it takes longer. That's what this library is for.

Pick your team. You'll find the work it does every week, from deal prep and QBRs to launch messaging and the board deck, and how to bring your company's own knowledge into each step: the approved positioning, the battlecards, the persona canvases, the customer quotes, the win/loss record.

It's inspiration, not a manual. 19 teams and 190 use cases still won't match exactly how your team works, so take a prompt, change it, and keep the version that works. Start with the one-line questions to see what Calven knows. Move to the workflow prompts when a task repeats; [prompt-patterns.md](prompt-patterns.md) explains both.
