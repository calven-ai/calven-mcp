# Customer success

**Use-case pages:** [`use-cases/customer-success/`](../use-cases/customer-success/)

## Who they are

Customer success managers, onboarding or implementation managers, and a CS lead or VP, reporting to a Chief Customer Officer, the COO or the CRO. Each CSM owns a book of accounts from signature to renewal: onboarding, adoption, value realization, risk, renewal and expansion. Measured on gross and net revenue retention, renewal rate, time to first value, adoption, expansion sourced and customer health.

## Responsibilities

**Onboarding and adoption**
- Run the sales-to-CS handoff: why the customer bought, who was in the deal, what was promised.
- Kickoff call, success plan with outcomes, milestones and owners, first value in the first 30 days.
- Adoption plans, training and enablement for the customer's users.

**Relationship and value**
- Regular check-ins with the day-to-day owner; QBRs with the business owner; EBRs with the executive sponsor once or twice a year.
- Stakeholder mapping and multi-threading so the account does not depend on one champion.
- Answering product, comparison and "what do we say" questions from the customer.

**Retention and growth**
- Health and risk reviews, save plays and escalation handling.
- Renewal prep and negotiation support, starting 90 to 180 days out.
- Expansion and upsell: spotting the signal, framing the case, handing to sales or closing it.
- Defending the account when a customer evaluates a competitor.

**Voice of the customer**
- Feeding product feedback, gaps and churn reasons back to product and marketing.
- Identifying advocates, references and case study candidates for customer marketing.

## Recurring activities

| Cadence | Activity |
|---|---|
| Daily | Customer questions, escalations, onboarding sessions, CRM notes |
| Weekly | Risk and renewal pipeline review with CS leadership and RevOps, check-ins with active onboarding accounts |
| Monthly | Health review of the book, adoption campaigns with customer marketing, retention and NRR review |
| Quarterly | QBRs, success plan refresh, renewal forecasting, feedback readout to product |
| Per account | Kickoff, success plan, EBR, renewal, expansion case, save play |

## Where Calven fits

Every account conversation starts with the same questions: why did they buy, who was in the room, what did they say they needed, what does our product actually do, what would the competitor they are looking at say. Calven holds the won deal with its drivers and buyer survey, the quotes from that account's calls, the contacts and their buying roles, the persona canvases for the people the CSM talks to, the product brief, and the battlecards. It does not hold product usage, support tickets, health scores or contract terms. Those steps are marked "Calven does not help here".
