# Customer success

Every account conversation starts with the same questions: why they bought, who was in the room, what they needed and what the competitor would say. Calven holds the won deal with its drivers and buyer survey, quotes from the account's calls, contacts and their buying roles, persona canvases, the product brief and the battlecards. Pull it into QBR or renewal prep from the AI tool you already use. Usage, tickets, health scores and contracts live elsewhere.
