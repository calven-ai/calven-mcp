# Leadership

Every board deck and plan has a page on the market and the competition, usually pieced together from whatever sales and marketing send the week before. Calven holds the approved version: win rate by competitor with sample sizes, loss reasons, the pipeline's ICP focus, positioning and proof points, competitor dossiers and recent moves, trends, opportunities and the customer's words. Ask for it from the AI tool you already use. Financials, hiring and cash stay with finance.
