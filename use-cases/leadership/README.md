# Leadership


## Who they are

The CEO, the executive team (COO, CRO, CMO, CPO, CFO, CTO) and the chief of staff who prepares their work. They own the company plan, the story the company tells investors, the board and employees, and the decisions nobody else can make: where to invest, what to stop, who to hire, which deals and partnerships to take. Measured on growth, retention, cash and whether the plan held. Their calendar runs on a quarterly rhythm: offsite, board meeting, retrospective, planning.

## Responsibilities

**Plan and strategy**
- Set the annual plan and quarterly priorities (OKRs), and keep the team on them week to week.
- Decide the ICP, the segments to win, and where to expand, with the executive team.
- Run the strategy offsite and the quarterly planning cycle.

**Board and investors**
- Prepare the board pack: CEO update, metrics, sales, product, marketing, strategic discussion items, risks.
- Send the monthly investor update; prepare the fundraising narrative and diligence materials.
- Answer the board's questions on competition, win rates, pipeline quality and the market.

**Company narrative**
- Tell the company story the same way to employees, customers, press and recruits: all-hands, the company narrative, the founding story, the category.
- Approve positioning and messaging when the stakes are company-wide.

**Market and competition**
- Stay current on competitors and the market without reading everything.
- Evaluate acquisitions, partnerships and channel deals.

**People**
- Hire senior leaders and brief candidates on the company, the buyer and the competition.
- Join escalations and the deals that need an executive.

**Operating rhythm**
- Weekly leadership meeting on a small set of indicators and cross-functional decisions.
- Monthly business review; quarterly business review and OKR reset.

## Recurring activities

| Cadence | Activity |
|---|---|
| Weekly | Leadership meeting, pipeline and metrics read, customer or prospect meetings on the deals that need an executive |
| Monthly | Investor update, business review, all-hands |
| Quarterly | Board pack and meeting, QBR, OKR planning, strategy offsite, competitive and market review |
| Annually | Annual plan and budget, company narrative refresh |
| Per event | Fundraise, acquisition or partnership evaluation, senior hire, executive escalation |

## Where Calven fits

Every one of these artifacts has a page about the market, the buyer, the competition or why deals are won and lost. Today those pages are built from whatever the CEO can get out of sales and marketing the week before the meeting. Calven holds the approved version: win rate by competitor with sample sizes, loss reasons, ICP focus of the pipeline, positioning and proof points, competitor dossiers and recent moves, trends and opportunities, and the customer's words. Financials, hiring and cash stay in finance and the board tool; Calven supplies the market side.
