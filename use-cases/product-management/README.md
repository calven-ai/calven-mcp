# Product management

Your hardest arguments are about evidence: which gap is costing deals, whether buyers care about a competitor's feature, and whether a launch claim holds up. Calven holds gaps ranked by deals at stake, deal drivers in the buyer's words, customer themes and quotes, competitor dossiers with feature comparisons, win/loss pricing verdicts, the product brief, and claims and drift findings. Ask for it from the AI tool you already use. Estimates, usage analytics and tickets stay where they are.
