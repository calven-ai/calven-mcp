# Advisory board prep

**Team:** Product management · also leadership, customer success, product marketing, customer marketing
**Impact:** Medium. A customer advisory board is for strategic input, not feature voting. The prep that makes it work is a pre-read grounded in what customers are saying, where the market is moving and what we are deciding. Calven holds the first two.
**Prerequisites:** call transcripts ingested (themes, quotes), market research run (trends, opportunities), win/loss surveys (gaps). CRM connected helps pick members.

## What the team is trying to do

Run a quarterly session where eight to fifteen senior customers give direction on product priorities, positioning and the market. Done means the member list, an agenda with two or three decisions to discuss, a pre-read with the evidence, and the questions that get strategic answers.

## The work, end to end

| # | Step | What the team does | Where Calven helps | Pulls from |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Pick members | Senior customers across segments | Accounts by fit tier and segment; contacts with exec sponsor or decision maker roles | CRM accounts, CRM contacts |
| 2 | Set the agenda | Two or three strategic questions | Top themes, product gaps, trends and opportunities with their so-what | Voice-of-customer dashboard, Insights overview (product gaps), trends, market opportunities |
| 3 | Write the pre-read | What we see, what we are considering | The evidence per agenda item | Themes, quotes, trends, competitor signals |
| 4 | Prepare the questions | Questions that invite direction, not votes | Persona canvases: goals, company initiatives | Persona |
| 5 | Run and capture | The session | Calven does not help here | |
| 6 | Follow up | What we heard and what we will do | If the session is ingested as a conversation: quotes and themes | Quotes, themes |

## Recommended prompts

### Step 2 and 3: agenda and pre-read

```
Using Calven MCP, draft the agenda and pre-read for the [quarter] customer advisory board.

CONTEXT
Eight to fifteen senior customers. I want two or three strategic topics, each with the evidence that made us pick it, and no feature voting.

PULL FROM THE UNIVERSE
- The top customer themes and movers this quarter, with quotes.
- The product gaps ranked by deals at stake.
- Trends and opportunities with the highest severity or impact, with their so-what.
- Recent competitor moves with high severity.

BUILD
- Three candidate topics, each with: why now (the evidence), what we are considering, the question for the board.
- A pre-read of one page per topic.

OUTPUT
The agenda and the pre-reads, sources inline.

GROUNDING
Use only themes, gaps, trends and signals in the Universe, cited with samples and dates. Do not include a topic the evidence does not support.

[name the quarter and any topic leadership has already set]
```

### Step 1: member selection

```
Using Calven MCP, propose members for the advisory board.

CONTEXT
I want coverage across [segments] with senior people who have a stake in where the product goes.

PULL FROM THE UNIVERSE
- Customer accounts by ICP fit tier, industry and size.
- Contacts at those accounts with role exec sponsor, economic buyer or decision maker.
- Which of those accounts raised strategic themes on calls.

BUILD
- A table: account, segment, contact and role, the theme they raised, why they belong.

OUTPUT
A member shortlist of twenty, grouped by segment.

GROUNDING
Use only accounts and contacts in the Universe. If the pipeline category is restricted, say so.

[name the segments]
```

### Step 4: the questions

```
Using Calven MCP, write the discussion questions for [topic].

CONTEXT
The board members are [personas]. I want questions that get direction on [topic], not feature requests.

PULL FROM THE UNIVERSE
- The personas' canvases: company objectives, goals and KPIs, jobs to be done.
- The quotes behind [topic].

WRITE
- Six questions that connect [topic] to their objectives and KPIs, each with the follow-up.

OUTPUT
The question list.

GROUNDING
Ground the questions in the canvases and quotes, cited.

[name the topic and the personas]
```

## Ad hoc questions

- Which strategic themes grew most this quarter?
- Which trends have the highest severity right now, and what is the so-what?
- Which customer accounts have an exec sponsor contact?
- Which product gaps have the most deals at stake?
- What are [persona]'s company objectives, according to the canvas?
- What did advisory board members say on calls since the last session?
- Which competitor moves this quarter would customers ask us about?
- What changed in the product since [date]?
- Write three discussion questions about [gap].

## Good practice

- Pick topics from evidence the board can react to: a trend, a gap with deals, a theme with momentum.
- Keep the pre-read to one page per topic with the quotes in it.
- Ingest the session recording so the follow-up has the board's words.
- Ask about their objectives, not our roadmap.

## Not covered today

- Scheduling, invitations, the session itself.
- Roadmap commitments made in the session. Those go to the roadmap tool.
