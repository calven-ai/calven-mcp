# Discovery interview prep

**Team:** Product management · also design, user research
**Impact:** Medium. The weekly customer interview is the keystone discovery habit. Calven makes each one count: the persona's jobs and pains as hypotheses, what this account already said, and a rehearsal against the persona before the call.
**Prerequisites:** personas approved (canvases). Better with call transcripts ingested (what the account said before) and CRM connected (who the contact is).

## What the team is trying to do

Walk into a discovery interview with hypotheses from the evidence, questions that test them, and knowledge of what this customer already told the company. Done means an interview guide per persona, a one-page brief per interviewee, and a rehearsal.

## The work, end to end

| # | Step | What the team does | Where Calven helps | Pulls from |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Pick the outcome and the persona | What the interview is meant to learn | Persona canvases: jobs, pains, goals | Persona |
| 2 | Form hypotheses | What we think is true | Themes and quotes from that persona; deal drivers | Themes, quotes, deal drivers |
| 3 | Write the guide | Open questions that test the hypotheses | The canvas and the quotes, for language | Persona, quotes |
| 4 | Brief on the interviewee | Who they are, what their company said before | Contact, account, prior conversations and quotes | CRM contacts, CRM accounts, customer conversations, quotes |
| 5 | Rehearse | Practise against the persona | Mock interview with the persona | Persona |
| 6 | Interview and synthesise | The call and its notes | Ingest the call; then quotes and themes | Quotes, themes |

## Recommended prompts

### Step 2 and 3: hypotheses and guide

```
Using Calven MCP, write the discovery interview guide for [persona] on [outcome].

CONTEXT
We interview one customer a week. This round is about [outcome]. I want hypotheses from our evidence and open questions that test them without leading.

PULL FROM THE UNIVERSE
- The [persona] canvas: jobs to be done, pains, goals and KPIs.
- Themes and quotes from this persona related to [outcome].
- Deal drivers that touch it.

BUILD
- Five hypotheses, each with the evidence behind it.
- Ten open questions in the customer's language, mapped to the hypotheses, with follow-ups.
- The three things we do not know at all.

OUTPUT
The interview guide.

GROUNDING
Ground hypotheses in the canvas, quotes and drivers, cited. Do not write questions that lead to our product.

[name the persona and the outcome]
```

### Step 4: the interviewee brief

```
Using Calven MCP, brief me on [contact] at [account] before the interview.

CONTEXT
I am interviewing [contact] tomorrow. I want to know their role, what their company has said to us before, and what not to ask again.

PULL FROM THE UNIVERSE
- The contact: title, role, lifecycle stage.
- The account: industry, size, fit tier, open and closed deals.
- Prior conversations with the account and the quotes from them.
- The persona that fits the contact's role.

BUILD
- Who they are and the persona that applies.
- What they or colleagues said before, with quotes.
- Three questions specific to them.

OUTPUT
A one-page brief.

GROUNDING
Use only the Universe, cited. If the pipeline category is restricted, brief from the persona and conversations only and say so.

[name the contact and the account]
```

### Step 5: the rehearsal

```
Using Calven MCP, rehearse the discovery interview with me as [persona].

CONTEXT
I will run the guide below. Play the persona: answer the way they would, go off track where they would, and push back where the question leads. After ten minutes, drop character and tell me which questions led the witness and which got nothing.

PULL FROM THE UNIVERSE
- The [persona] canvas.

ROLE-PLAY
- Stay in character. Use their pains and vocabulary.
- Debrief: leading questions, dead questions, the follow-up I missed.

OUTPUT
The role-play, then the debrief.

GROUNDING
Stay true to the canvas. Do not make the persona more forthcoming than the profile supports.

[paste the guide]
```

## Ad hoc questions

- What are [persona]'s jobs to be done?
- Which pains does [persona] rate high impact?
- What has [account] said on calls before?
- Who at [account] is an end user?
- What do customers in [persona] say about [topic]? Quotes.
- Which hypotheses about [outcome] does our evidence already support?
- How does [persona] describe success in their own words?
- Which deal drivers in the Capability category touched [outcome] this year?
- Play [persona] and let me run my first three interview questions on you.

## Good practice

- Form hypotheses from quotes, then write questions that could disprove them.
- Read the account's prior conversations so you do not ask what they already told a colleague.
- Rehearse once per guide, not per interview.
- Ingest the interview so the next guide starts from it.
