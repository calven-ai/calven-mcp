# Sales leadership

You decide on numbers that already live in the CRM and on reasons that only live in buyers' heads. Calven holds both: win rate by segment, competitor and persona with sample sizes, pipeline in and out of profile, what buyers said decided each deal, how reps pitch, and this month's competitor moves. Ask for it from the AI tool you already use. Quota, comp, activity and the forecast stay in your CRM and forecasting tool.
