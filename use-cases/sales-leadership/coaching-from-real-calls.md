# Coaching from real calls

**Team:** Sales leadership · also sales enablement, product marketing
**Impact:** High. A manager coaches ten reps on what they heard once; the calls hold what reps actually say, which pillars they pull through, which objections they fumble and what buyers answered. Coaching from that evidence beats coaching from memory.
**Prerequisites:** call transcripts ingested (vendor quotes, customer quotes, conversations), messaging approved. Better with win/loss surveys running (what buyers said about the sales team, sales-process drivers).

## What the team is trying to do

Run one-on-ones and team coaching on evidence: how each rep opens, discovers, claims value and handles objections, compared with the approved messaging and with what buyers said in surveys. Done means a coaching note per rep with their own words, the gap against the story, and one thing to practise. Without the company's data coaching is the manager's recollection of the last call they joined.

## The work, end to end

| # | Step | What the team does | Where Calven helps | Pulls from |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Pick the rep and the window | Last month's calls | Conversations by account and date with the rep's quotes | Customer conversations, vendor quotes |
| 2 | See how they pitch | Value claims, differentiation, proof, discovery questions | The rep's vendor quotes by category, and whether each pulls through an approved pillar | Vendor quotes, messaging dashboard (field adoption) |
| 3 | See what buyers said back | Objections, pains, competitor mentions on the same calls | Customer quotes from the same conversations | Quotes |
| 4 | Check objection handling | Did the rep answer the objection the approved way | Approved objection handling against what the rep said | Messaging (objection handling), vendor quotes |
| 5 | Add the buyer's verdict | What surveyed buyers said about the sales process on this rep's deals | Sales team survey section and sales-process drivers on the rep's deals | Win/loss (surveys: sales team), deal drivers |
| 6 | Write the coaching note | Strength, gap, one practice | Calven supplies the evidence, the manager writes the note | |
| 7 | Team patterns | Which gaps are everyone's | Pillar pull-through and objections missed across all reps | Messaging dashboard, vendor quotes |
| 8 | Practise | Role-play the gap | Role-play as the persona | Persona canvas |

## Recommended prompts

### Steps 1 to 5: the coaching note for one rep

```
Using Calven MCP, prepare my coaching note for [rep] from their calls in [window].

CONTEXT
One-on-one on [day]. I want evidence from their own calls, not my memory.

PULL FROM THE UNIVERSE
- [rep]'s vendor quotes in the window by category: value claims, differentiation, proof points, discovery questions, objection handling, pricing, caveats.
- Which approved messaging pillars those claims pull through, and which pillars never appear.
- The customer quotes from the same conversations: objections, competitor mentions, pains.
- Our approved objection handling for the objections raised.
- What surveyed buyers said about the sales team on [rep]'s deals, and the sales-process drivers.

BUILD
- Strengths: two things [rep] says that match the approved story or that buyers praised, verbatim.
- Gaps: the pillar they never use, the objection they answered off-script (their words against the approved line), a claim not in the product brief if any.
- The buyer's verdict in one line, if surveyed.
- One thing to practise this week.

OUTPUT
A one-page coaching note with the call and quote behind every point.

GROUNDING
Use only quotes, messaging and survey data in the Universe, verbatim and cited. Do not judge what the transcripts do not show. If the window holds too few calls, say so.

[name the rep and the window]
```

### Step 7: team patterns

```
Using Calven MCP, what are the team-wide coaching gaps this quarter?

PULL FROM THE UNIVERSE
- Pillar pull-through across all reps from the messaging field adoption read.
- Objections raised most on calls, and the vendor quotes answering them, against the approved objection handling.
- Value claims reps make that are not in the messaging or the product brief.

BUILD
- The pillar the team under-uses and who uses it well.
- The objection most often answered off-script, with the approved line.
- The off-brief claims to stop.

OUTPUT
A half page for the team meeting, with figures and quotes.

GROUNDING
Use only the Universe, cited with samples. Name reps only where the quote is theirs.
```

### Step 8: practise the gap

```
Using Calven MCP, run a role-play for [rep] on [objection] as [persona].

CONTEXT
[rep] answered this objection off-script on [number] calls. Play the buyer, raise it the way the canvas says, and after the exchange give the approved line with the evidence behind it.

PULL FROM THE UNIVERSE
- The [persona] canvas and the approved objection handling for [objection].
- A won deal where this objection was overcome, with the buyer's words.

OUTPUT
An interactive exchange, then the approved line and the proof.

GROUNDING
Stay true to the persona and the approved handling in the Universe.

[name the rep, the objection and the persona]
```

### Step 2: one rep's discovery

```
Using Calven MCP, show me the discovery questions [rep] asked in [window] and compare them with the battlecard's.

PULL FROM THE UNIVERSE
- [rep]'s vendor quotes of category Discovery question.
- The discovery and qualifying questions in the battlecards for the competitors on their deals.

OUTPUT
Their questions verbatim, the battlecard questions they never asked, and the one to add.

GROUNDING
Verbatim and cited; do not invent questions the transcripts do not hold.

[name the rep and window]
```

## Ad hoc questions

- What value claims did [rep] make on calls last month? Quote them.
- Which messaging pillar does the team use least on calls?
- How did [rep] answer "[objection]" and what is the approved line?
- Which reps pull through the [pillar] pillar most?
- What did buyers say about our sales process in the last ten surveys?
- Did [rep] claim anything that is not in the product brief?
- Which discovery questions show up on won calls but not on [rep]'s?
- What objections came up on [rep]'s calls that our messaging does not cover?
- Which caveats do reps give that we should check?
- How often do reps mention [competitor] unprompted?

## Good practice

- Give a window with enough calls. Ask the AI tool to say how many conversations it found before trusting a pattern.
- Keep the note to one practice item. Evidence makes the gap undeniable; one item makes it fixable.
- Use verbatim quotes in the one-on-one. A rep hears their own words differently from a manager's summary.
- Pair every gap with the approved line and the proof, so the fix is the current story and not the manager's version.
- Run the team-pattern read monthly and feed off-brief claims to enablement and PMM.

## Not covered today

- Call recording, transcription and call scoring. Calven reads transcripts once they are ingested.
- Activity metrics and talk ratios.
- Writing coaching notes into a performance system.
