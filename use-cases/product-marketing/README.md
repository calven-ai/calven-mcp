# Product marketing

Every deliverable you ship is a synthesis of the company's market, buyer, product and deal knowledge. Calven's agents keep that knowledge current and you approve it. Ask the AI tool you already use and it turns the approved version into a battlecard one-pager, a persona brief, a win/loss readout or a fact-check of a page before it ships. The judgment stays with you: which story to tell, what to approve and what to say no to.
