# Sales enablement

Almost everything you build sits downstream of what product marketing knows. Calven holds the approved positioning, messaging, personas, battlecards, win/loss and customer voice, plus what happens on calls: customer quotes, rep quotes, objections and deal drivers. Ask the AI tool you already use to turn it into training, quizzes and coaching notes, or to check what reps say against the current story. Your LMS, content library and call recorder stay put.

## Use cases

| Use case | What it gets you |
|---|---|
| [What changed this week](what-changed-this-week.md) | The weekly field digest: competitor moves, new objections, stale talk track, updated assets |
| [Call coaching](call-coaching.md) | Coaching from real calls: the objections reps hear, whether the talk track covers them, what the rep said versus the approved story |
| [Talk track audit](talk-track-audit.md) | Whether reps say the pillars, where the talk track no longer holds, what to retrain |
| [Onboarding plans](onboarding-plans.md) | A 30-60-90 plan and the reading, from the approved story |
| [Objection libraries](objection-libraries.md) | The objections buyers raise, ranked by frequency and deals touched, with evidence-backed answers |

| [Launch readiness](launch-readiness.md) | Field FAQ, talk track, objections and certification for a release, from the product brief and positioning |
