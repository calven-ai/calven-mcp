# Sales enablement

**Pages:** [`use-cases/sales-enablement/`](../use-cases/sales-enablement/)

## Who they are

An enablement manager or a small team (often one person under 200 employees), reporting to the VP Sales, the CRO or marketing. They own the rep's readiness: onboarding, training, coaching programs, the content reps sell with, and the tools that deliver it. Measured on ramp time, time to first deal, certification pass rate, content use in won deals, win rate and quota attainment. In companies without a dedicated role, the work is split between a PMM, a sales manager and RevOps.

## Responsibilities

**Onboarding**
- Run the new-hire program from day one to full ramp: product, market, personas, competitors, methodology, tools.
- Set the 30-60-90 milestones and the certification that marks a rep as ramped.

**Training and certification**
- Product launch training, methodology reinforcement, skill sessions, sales kickoffs, quarterly refreshers.
- Certify reps on the pitch, the demo, objection handling and competitive plays; retest when the story changes.

**Sales content**
- Build, maintain and distribute what reps sell with: battlecards, one-pagers, case studies, talk tracks, objection libraries, FAQs, persona cheat sheets.
- Keep it current and retire what is stale; know what gets used in deals.

**Coaching**
- Give managers the structure to coach: call reviews, scorecards, role-play, deal reviews.
- Spot the patterns across calls: objections reps hear, where the talk track fails, which pillars reps actually say.

**Field communication**
- The weekly digest of what changed: competitor moves, new objections, updated assets, product releases.
- Launch readiness: the field is trained and equipped before anything goes live.

**Measurement and tools**
- Track ramp, certification, content adoption and win rate; own the enablement platform, call recording and the content library.

## Recurring activities

| Cadence | Activity |
|---|---|
| Daily | Answer field questions, update assets, review calls flagged by managers |
| Weekly | Field digest, enablement session (30 to 60 minutes), call reviews with managers, new-hire check-ins |
| Monthly | Product update training, certification cohort, content audit, adoption review |
| Quarterly | Kickoff or refresher on positioning and competitive plays, win rate and ramp review, content retirement |
| Per launch | Readiness plan, training, FAQ, demo script, certification |
| Per hire | Onboarding plan, buddy pairing, 30-60-90 review |

## Where Calven fits

Almost everything enablement produces is downstream of what PMM knows: positioning, messaging, personas, battlecards, win/loss and customer voice. Calven holds that as the approved version, plus the evidence of what happens on calls (customer quotes, rep quotes, objections, deal drivers). From their AI tool, enablement builds training, quizzes, one-pagers and coaching notes from the current story and checks what reps say against it. The delivery (LMS, content library, call recording platform, Slack) stays where it is.
