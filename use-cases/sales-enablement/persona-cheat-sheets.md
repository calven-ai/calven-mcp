# Persona cheat sheets

**Team:** Sales enablement · also BDRs, account executives, product marketing
**Impact:** Medium. A one-page persona sheet is what a rep reads before a first call. Built from the approved canvas, it tells them what the buyer is measured on, what hurts, what they object to and what to ask, without a workshop.
**Prerequisites:** personas approved (rows and canvases). Better with call transcripts ingested (quotes per persona) and CRM connected (win rate by persona).

## What the team is trying to do

Give reps one page per persona they can use in the five minutes before a call. Done means a sheet per approved persona with KPIs, pains, objections, hooks, discovery questions and the proof that works, refreshed when the canvas changes. Without the company's own knowledge the sheet is a job title and three adjectives.

## The work, end to end

| # | Step | What the team does | Where Calven helps | Pulls from |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | List the personas | Decide which get a sheet | Approved personas by type and buying role | Personas |
| 2 | Draft the sheet | Condense the canvas for a rep | KPIs, pains, gains, jobs, objections with responses, hooks, watering holes | Persona canvas |
| 3 | Add the evidence | Real words and what works | Quotes from this persona on calls; win rate by persona; drivers that decided deals where this persona was the contact | Quotes, persona dashboard, deal drivers |
| 4 | Add the discovery questions | What to ask first | Questions from the canvas's jobs and pains; the battlecard discovery questions where a competitor is likely | Persona canvas, battlecards |
| 5 | Publish and refresh | Distribute, update when the canvas changes | Calven does not publish; document versions show when a canvas changed | List documents |

## Recommended prompts

### Step 2 to 4: the sheet

```
Using Calven MCP, write the rep cheat sheet for [persona].

CONTEXT
One page a rep reads before a call. Sections: what they are measured on, the three pains, the three objections with our answer, three hooks, five discovery questions, the proof that works, where they learn.

PULL FROM THE UNIVERSE
- The [persona] canvas: KPIs, pains, gains, jobs to be done, objections, messaging hooks, watering holes.
- Customer quotes from this persona's role on calls in the last two quarters, by category.
- The persona dashboard's win rate when this persona is engaged, with n.
- Deal drivers with direction helped on deals where this persona was the primary contact.

WRITE
The sheet in the sections above, with a verbatim quote under pains and under proof, and the source per section.

OUTPUT
The sheet.

GROUNDING
Write only from the canvas, quotes and dashboard, cited. Do not add traits the canvas does not state. If the canvas is not published, say so and use the row attributes only.

[name the persona]
```

### Review mode: check a sheet someone else wrote

```
Using Calven MCP, check this persona sheet against the approved canvas.

CONTEXT
Below is a cheat sheet in use. I want every statement checked against the current canvas and the quotes.

PULL FROM THE UNIVERSE
- The [persona] canvas and the recent quotes from this persona.

CHECK
- Each statement: supported (cite), contradicted (give the canvas line), or not in the canvas.
- Objections on recent calls the sheet does not cover.

OUTPUT
The annotated sheet, then the lines to change.

GROUNDING
Judge only against the Universe. Do not pass a statement because it sounds plausible.

[paste the sheet and name the persona]
```

### Gap mode: personas without a sheet, and sheets without evidence

```
Using Calven MCP, tell me which personas need a sheet most.

CONTEXT
Below is the list of personas we have sheets for. I want the gaps ranked by how often the persona appears in deals.

PULL FROM THE UNIVERSE
- Approved personas by buying role.
- The persona dashboard: buying group presence, win rate by persona, contact coverage.

BUILD
- Personas with no sheet, ranked by deal presence and win-rate impact, with n.

OUTPUT
The ranked gap list.

GROUNDING
Rank by dashboard numbers with n and window. Personas with too few deals are listed with "below floor", not skipped.

[paste the list of personas with sheets]
```

## Ad hoc questions

- What is [persona] measured on?
- What are [persona]'s top three pains, with a quote?
- What objections does [persona] raise and how do we answer?
- Give me five discovery questions for [persona].
- Where does [persona] learn: events, communities, publications?
- What is our win rate when [persona] is on the deal?
- Which hook lands with [persona]?
- Which personas are buyers versus stakeholders versus users?
- What does [persona] say about [competitor]?

## Good practice

- One persona per sheet, named as Calven names it.
- Put a verbatim quote under every pain. Reps believe the buyer, not the sheet.
- Add the win rate with n so reps know which persona to multi-thread to.
- Check the canvas version before a refresh; most sheets go stale because the canvas changed and nobody noticed.
- Pair the sheet with the battlecard's discovery questions when a competitor is likely.

## Not covered today

- Contact-level research on a named person. The CRM contact row gives title and role; anything else is outside Calven.
- Publishing and distributing the sheet.
